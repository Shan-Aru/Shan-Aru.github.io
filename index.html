Generative AIGroup project

Exam Prep Assistant

Upload your course PDFs and previous years' question papers. It answers questions from your notes with page references, finds the main topics, and predicts which questions are likely to come up.

  • RAG over your notes with LangChain, FAISS and sentence-transformers, with cited sources
  • Topic extraction with TF-IDF and key-phrase scoring, plus an LLM summary
  • Scores each topic on how often, how recently and how heavily it was asked, then writes likely questions
